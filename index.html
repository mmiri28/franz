Chapter 1

The Plan

Francis had seen one photo of a sunrise from a mountaintop, and that was enough. "This weekend," he announced to everyone in the group chat, "I will stand above the clouds and watch the sun rise like a king."

His preparation was thorough. He bought a headlamp. He bought a second headlamp in case the first one got lonely. He packed three sandwiches, a flask of tea, and a folding chair, because kings do not stand for long.

What he did not pack was a map. "Maps are for people without instinct," he said.

Chapter 2

The Alarm

The sunrise was at 6:42. The hike took two hours. Francis, a mathematician at heart, set his alarm for 4:00.

Unfortunately, he set it for 4:00 PM.

He woke at 5:51 anyway, thanks to a neighbour's very dedicated rooster, and leapt out of bed with the energy of a man who has made a terrible mistake and refuses to admit it. "No problem," he told the rooster. "I will simply walk faster."

Chapter 3

The Bus

Francis caught the first bus he saw, on the reasonable logic that all buses go somewhere and mountains are somewhere.

Forty minutes later, the bus stopped at a goat farm. The driver turned around. "Last stop," he said. Francis looked out the window. There was a hill. It was not a mountain, but it was trying its best.

"This is the one," Francis declared, and got off with his folding chair.

Chapter 4

The Mountain

The climb was brutal. It took eleven minutes. Halfway up, a goat began following him. Francis named it Captain and gave it half a sandwich, which turned out to be a strategic error, because Captain now considered himself a member of the expedition and wanted the other two sandwiches as payment.

At the top, Francis unfolded his chair, switched on both headlamps, and poured his tea. Captain stood beside him like a loyal knight. The sky was beginning to glow.

Chapter 5

The Sunrise

The sun rose. It was magnificent. It was golden. It was, Francis slowly realised, rising directly behind him.

He had set up his chair facing west.

Captain ate the last sandwich while Francis turned the chair around. By the time he sat back down, the sun was fully up and very bright and not doing anything special anymore.

Francis took a selfie with Captain anyway. He posted it to the group chat with one caption:

"Stood above the clouds. Watched the sunrise like a king. Made a new friend. 10/10 expedition, would plan again."

Nobody had the heart to point out that there were no clouds, and the hill was 300 metres tall.
